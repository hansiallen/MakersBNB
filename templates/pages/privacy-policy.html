Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2024

Introduction

Welcome to MakersBnB! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services. By using MakersBnB, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information from users when they:

  • Create an account or profile
  • Make a booking or listing
  • Contact customer support
  • Use the website and its features

The information we may collect includes:

  • Personal details (name, email, phone number)
  • Payment information (credit card details, billing address)
  • Location data (IP address, geolocation data for bookings)
  • Usage data (activity on the platform, preferences, interactions)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, personalize, and improve our services
  • To process bookings and payments
  • To communicate with you regarding your account, bookings, and updates
  • To respond to customer service inquiries
  • To send marketing communications (you can opt-out at any time)

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with the following:

  • Service providers who assist with our operations (e.g., payment processors, hosting providers)
  • Business partners for collaborative promotions or events
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no system can be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us through the contact information below.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on our website. These technologies help us understand how you use the platform, customize content, and improve functionality. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Children's Privacy

MakersBnB is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child under 18, we will delete it immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the date of the last update will be indicated at the top of the policy.
